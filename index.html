An Art Illiterate’s Guide to Art History at the Met

No background needed. Walk one route through the museum, in the order art actually happened. The 3D map is built from the Met’s own floor plan, follows you from work to work, and every masterpiece has a card that explains it.

Galleries traced from the Met’s official map · Fifth Ave along the front edge

Independent guide, not affiliated with The Metropolitan Museum of Art. Gallery footprints are traced from the Met’s interactive map, and gallery numbers come from the Met’s collection data (October 2026). Works move and galleries close: the modern wing is closed for construction of the Tang Wing (planned 2030), and the Ancient West Asian and Cypriot galleries reopen in 2027. Check the Met’s official map before you go.
